Who We Are

A Strong Pillar for Access to Justice

Neupane Law House (न्यौपाने ल हाउस) is a legal practice based in Kathmandu, Nepal, committed to providing professional legal services with honesty, responsibility and dedication.

With 12 years of court practice, our work encompasses judicial and quasi-judicial proceedings, legal drafting, public interest litigation, mediation, arbitration, corporate matters and legal advisory services.

Our mission is to support access to justice and protect legal rights while maintaining transparency and professional integrity.

01

Litigation & Court Representation

Representation, pleading and case preparation before district courts, high courts, the Supreme Court and competent judicial bodies.

02

Legal Drafting & Research

Drafting petitions, pleadings, contracts and legal documents, together with legal research, investigation and advisory opinions.

03

Public Interest Litigation

Legal action involving public rights, social justice, consumer interests, public health and matters of wider community concern.

04

Mediation & Arbitration

Assistance in resolving civil, commercial and other eligible disputes through mediation, negotiation and arbitration.

Corporate & Business Law

06

Tax & Financial Law

Legal Counseling

08

Notarial Services

Justice & Social Issues

Advocacy against injustice, protection of legal rights and legal initiatives involving social values and community welfare.
